# Sample Competency Framework

## Individual Contributor Tier
* Action orientation: Delivering high-quality individual work efficiently and meeting project timelines reliably.
* Collaborative communication: Sharing ideas transparently, listening actively, and working constructively across immediate team circles.
* Problem solving: Identifying tactical roadblocks and implementing immediate, practical solutions within a defined scope.
* Functional expertise: Demonstrating core technical or specialized skills required to execute day-to-day responsibilities.
* Personal accountability: Owning individual deliverables, mistakes, and personal professional development goals.

## Manager Tier (Includes all individual contributor competencies)
* Performance enablement: Coaching direct reports, providing actionable feedback, and unblocking career growth paths.
* Resource optimization: Allocating time, budget, and tools effectively to maximize team output without burnout.
* Conflict resolution: Identifying, addressing, and resolving interpersonal or operational friction within the team early.
* Operational execution: Translating high-level company strategies into clear, measurable milestones for the team.
* Empathetic leadership: Prioritizing team psychological safety, active listening, and individual well-being.

## Director Tier (Includes all individual contributor and manager competencies)
* Strategic foresight: Anticipating industry shifts, market trends, and long-term organizational opportunities to set vision.
* Stakeholder influence: Aligning cross-functional executive peers and driving consensus on major organizational initiatives.
* Change management: Leading large-scale structural, technical, or cultural transformations smoothly across departments.
* Talent architecture: Designing long-term workforce capabilities, succession plans, and organizational structures.
* Fiscal governance: Managing macro budgets, evaluating ROI on large initiatives, and ensuring long-term financial health.
